About SolClear AI

We are a UAE-based initiative dedicated to maximizing solar efficiency in the world’s most challenging environments. SolClear AI is driven by an HCT student team focused on delivering data-driven clarity to solar farm operators across the region.

Our mission is to empower governments and enterprises with actionable insight, reducing maintenance costs, optimizing cleaning schedules, and extending the life of critical renewable infrastructure.

What’s SolClear?

SolClear AI unites AI-powered image analysis with real-time electrical data to detect dust accumulation and quantify performance impact across photovoltaic arrays.

The SolClear AI project combines a Raspberry Pi 5, a high-resolution camera, and Arduino-based voltage and current sensors to capture synchronized visuals and electrical readings throughout the day. By labeling images with measured power losses, the system trains machine learning models that can classify dust levels and predict energy impact automatically, reducing manual inspections and extending asset life for solar installations across the UAE.

Camera-Captured Reality

A high-resolution camera mounted to a representative panel watches for dust, soiling, and shading—no satellite imagery required. Because dust settles evenly across arrays, a single vantage point provides actionable insight for every string.

Sensor Fusion Intelligence

A Raspberry Pi 5 synchronizes camera frames with Arduino-powered voltage and current sensors, creating a labeled dataset that links visual dust levels to real power losses throughout the day.

Predictive Operations

Machine learning models trained on synchronized imagery and electrical data forecast cleaning windows, energy recovery, and maintenance priorities—turning manual inspections into automated intelligence.
