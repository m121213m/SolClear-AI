About SolClear AI

We are a UAE-based team dedicated to maximizing solar efficiency in the world’s most challenging environments. SolClear AI was founded by Mohammed Abdulla and Sulaiman Majd to deliver data-driven clarity to solar farm operators across the region.

Our mission is to empower governments and enterprises with actionable insight, reducing maintenance costs, optimizing cleaning schedules, and extending the life of critical renewable infrastructure.

Our Values

  • Innovation: Purpose-built AI models trained on Middle East dust patterns.
  • Reliability: Resilient monitoring that thrives in heat, sand, and salt.
  • Partnership: Tailored engagements for government agencies and utility providers.

What’s SolClear?

SolClear AI is an end-to-end monitoring suite combining computer vision, IoT telemetry, and predictive maintenance workflows designed for solar assets.

Computer Vision Insight

Our AI models analyze high-resolution imagery to detect dust coverage, hotspots, and panel degradation—long before it impacts your energy output.

Actionable Efficiency Metrics

Receive automated alerts with quantified energy loss projections and cleaning recommendations, so teams can schedule interventions precisely when needed.

Scalable Integrations

SolClear connects with existing SCADA systems and asset management platforms, delivering trusted data streams for executives, engineers, and field crews.
